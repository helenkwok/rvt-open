    File status

    For Revit 2014 to 2027 files, as the command-line IFC export does: every element Revit saved a mesh for, doors and windows among them. Off keeps each one's bounding box and uses less memory. Changing this reopens the open file.

    The phase the model and its exports show, as rvt-ifc --phase chooses it; the last is the default. An element that does not exist in the phase, and a room whose own phase is not it, are left out. Where the last phase holds no room, the default shows the rooms of the latest phase that holds any; choosing a phase other than the default shows exactly that phase. Changing this reopens the open file.

          How to use it
          
    
            Open locally or pick a sample → read the Decode and Export rows above →
            inspect the decoded elements → choose an IFC bar in the Export menu (scaffold is the
            honest bar for synthetic files) → export after checking the quality label → download
            diagnostics for partial exports. Typed elements come from Revit 2023, 2024 and 2025
            projects, and experimentally from Revit 2008 to 2022, 2026 and 2027 projects; other
            files export a scaffold.
          
    
        
    
        
    
          Supported profile
        
    
        
    
          
    
            Formats
            .rvt, .rfa, .rte, .rft — metadata/schema across verified Revit 2016–2026 family samples.
          
    
          
    
            Real projects
            Core Interior (2024) opens from the gallery with walls, slabs, columns, doors, windows, rooms and real storeys, and Einhoven (2023) with its walls and storeys. Both come from magnetar-io/revit-test-datasets (MIT) and are the files the CI browser regression gates.
          
    
          
    
            Synthetics
            The three synthetic sample files (architectural-2024, structural-2023, mep-2024) open cleanly but decode as scaffold ~25%. That is expected, not a fault.
          
    
          
    
            IFC now
            Revit 2024 and 2025 projects export walls, doors, windows, columns, slabs, roofs, ceilings, stairs, railings, rooms, curtain-wall parts, furniture, casework, lighting and plumbing fixtures, equipment, ducts, pipes, structural framing and foundations, and site elements, each under the ElementId and, for family instances, the Family:Type name Revit's own export gives it. Bodies are bounding boxes, refined to sketched outlines for slabs, roofs and stair landings, roof planes along their slopes, trimmed runs for walls, beams along their axes, cylinders for pipes, and treads and risers for stair flights, spiral ones included; doors and windows are drawn from the meshes Revit saved for them where those decode whole. Revit 2023 projects export typed elements too, and Revit 2008 to 2022, 2026 and 2027 projects experimentally; other files export a valid scaffold. The typed / geometry / strict bars refuse a file that does not meet them.
          
    
          
    
            3-D view
            Revit 2024 and 2025 walls, floors, roofs and ceilings are drawn as their layers, each in its material's shading colour, where the layers add up to the element's thickness. Other elements take their category's colour.
          
    
          
    
            Main target
            Revit 2024/2025 projects: levels, walls, floors, doors, windows, rooms, materials, and the building products around them.
          
    
          
    
            Not ready
            Element records outside Revit 2023 to 2025, which export only experimentally; true element geometry beyond bounding boxes and sketched outlines for most categories; most parameters; layer sets for elements drawn whole or sloped; some elements Revit's own export leaves out, such as opening-cut families and curtain panels with no resolved type, which rvt-open still exports; and semantic Revit editing.
