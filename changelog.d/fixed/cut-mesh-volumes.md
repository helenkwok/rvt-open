- **A body a void cuts, or whose faces meet at T-junctions, gets its own
  volume.** A cut split faces on one side of an edge and not the other, so
  the body was drawn on both sides and its NetVolume summed one tetrahedron
  to the origin per triangle: a published Ionic column's abacus wrote
  1.284478 m³ where its mesh holds 0.017736. The cut now splits those edges,
  and a faceted body is measured as written, its closed parts for volume and
  its open parts for area only. On 633 published course families 426
  NetVolumes move (every value before was off its mesh; after, 389 equal
  it); on the 27 public models 2,188 move in 17 models (1,731 closed bodies
  now equal their mesh, 229 open meshes give none). No overall size moves.
