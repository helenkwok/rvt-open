- **Surfaces of revolution and ruled faces of one connected group draw their
  shared edges through the same points.** A face took its neighbour's grid
  without the points that neighbour had itself taken, so a point a middle face
  took from one side did not reach the face on its other side, and two
  surfaces of revolution side by side exchanged nothing. The exchange now runs
  over the whole group of faces until no face takes a new point. It covers
  edges that run along one parameter in both faces; an edge that crosses both
  parameters of either face, a surface of revolution whose trim is not one
  rectangle, and two ruled faces neither trimmed to a rectangle keep their own
  points.
