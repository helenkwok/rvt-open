- **A cylinder face whose ring of stored edges joins within 0.00001 ft along the surface is drawn, not refused as a disconnected boundary.** A cylinder face's edges that did not meet within 1e-7 in the surface's parameters made the face refuse, and the element fall back to its box. Now a ring where every gap's step along the surface (a helix, so a step across the seam is a whole turn and never qualifies) is under 0.00001 ft joins: each such pair of ends is one point, the next edge's stored end is dropped, and no edge is added. A ring with an edge shorter than that, or whose gap could join at either end, stays refused, as does a face whose surface is not a cylinder; wider gaps stay refused. Plane faces already join up to 0.0005 ft and are unchanged. Measured on the 27 public models with a Revit export, `main` against this change, both scored by the same scorer: 25 exports are identical to the byte but for their timestamps, and the other two (the BIM4LCA housing with concrete terrain, the Snowdon architectural model) gain three bodies that were boxes, two terrain elements (volume 0.9990 and 1.0026 of Revit's) and a flow terminal (1.0027); no body that was drawn changes, and none is lost.
