- **A surface of revolution whose trim is not one rectangle and a rectangular
  one beside it draw their shared edge through the same points.** The trimmed
  surface kept its own points, so where its rectangular neighbour had taken
  points from the faces beyond it, their shared edge was drawn through
  different points. The two now take each other's points; two trimmed
  surfaces of revolution still exchange nothing. On the 27 public models with
  Revit's own export, the pairs of one trimmed and one rectangular surface of
  revolution meeting at a point only one of them draws fall from 554 to 0, and
  those of two trimmed ones from 1,536 to 1,160; directed edges without their
  reverse fall from 2,065,164 to 2,055,267, with 0.8% more triangles, and no
  body scores worse.
