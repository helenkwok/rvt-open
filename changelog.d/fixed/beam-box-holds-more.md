- **A beam whose record box holds more than the beam is drawn from its saved
  mesh.** A beam drawn along its location line takes its section from its
  record box; where that section is more than five times as wide or as deep
  as the beam's own saved mesh across the line, and the mesh decodes whole,
  the beam is drawn from the mesh instead, as a box is. A box that holds a
  wall-height section around a beam a few hundred millimetres deep drew the
  beam as a wall-sized sheet. On the public samples no beam is over 4.43
  times its mesh's depth, so none of them changes.
