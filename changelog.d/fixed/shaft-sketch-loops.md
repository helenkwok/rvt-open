- **A shaft opening cuts the floors and ceilings it crosses along its sketch.**
  A shaft was cut as its record box, so a shaft sketched as several loops cut
  everything between them: on the advanced architecture sample (Revit 2024 to
  2027) shaft 156935, four rectangles in a 189.5 by 176.1 ft box, left 4,800.9
  of the 16,746.2 ft² Revit gives each of the hollow-core floors 139803 and
  139829, and two stair landings nothing. On Revit 2024 to 2027 each loop of
  the shaft's sketch that meets a floor or ceiling is cut from it, and a
  ceiling it crosses is cut as a floor is (a shaft whose sketch is not read
  keeps its box in floors and cuts no ceiling): the two floors are now Revit's
  Area to 0.1 ft², and the furred ceilings 242937 and 247617, 719 ft² over it,
  are now within 0.1 ft².
