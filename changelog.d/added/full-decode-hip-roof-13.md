- **Line weight and colour of text and annotation styles.** A text, tag,
  dimension, spot, level, contour label or filled region type's line
  weight and colour are read from its category element's first graphics
  style; `field_parameters::LINKED_RULES` paths may hop more than once and
  take a list's first item. On `1-hip-roof` (Revit 2024) 560 more values
  match and values with no source fall from 6,396 to 5,836.
