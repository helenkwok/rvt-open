- **Text styles, family codes and view settings through another element.**
  `field_parameters::LINKED_RULES` read a parameter through an id into
  another element (a text type's font element, a family type's family, a
  viewer's view), and 39 more field rules join `FIELD_RULES`; a structural
  asset's unit weight is its density times standard gravity. On
  `1-hip-roof` (Revit 2024) parameter values with no source fall from
  7,929 to 6,396 and differing values from 160 to 127.
