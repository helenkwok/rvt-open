- **Standalone family revolutions:** where a family file's own forms draw
  no body, the scaffold export decodes a single visible positive revolution
  of a Revit 2020 family and writes it as an analytic
  `IfcRevolvedAreaSolid`: line profiles with full, partial and tilted
  placements, and arc profiles with a full turn only. Project instances,
  voids and other releases are outside this coverage.
